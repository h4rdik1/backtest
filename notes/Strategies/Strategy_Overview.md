# 🎯 Strategy Reference Notes

Detailed breakdowns of strategy mechanics used by the system:

## 1. CISD (Change in State of Delivery)
- Occurs after liquidity sweep at key highs/lows.
- Confirmed by body close past the opposing candle high/low.

## 2. FVG (Fair Value Gap)
- 3-candle pattern with imbalance gap between Candle 1 high and Candle 3 low (for bullish).

## 3. SFP (Swing Failure Pattern)
- Price sweeps previous high/low but fails to close beyond it.
